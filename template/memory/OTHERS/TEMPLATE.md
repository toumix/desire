# `OTHERS/<person>.md`

One standing note per collaborator, rewritten when they are re-read — not per turn. What they own,
what they have landed, what of theirs touches our lanes, and what they are waiting on from USER.

**It is written every time a session reviews a pull request that is not ours**, alongside that
item's `WORK/` note: the note says what the item is and dies when it merges, this says what working
with that person has been like and outlives every one of their pull requests.

The board carries only the part that changes an agent's work and links here for the rest; what
USER has to do about them is a section of `USER_TODO.md`, not here.

```markdown
# <login> — <name>, <what they own in one line>

**Last read** <date>, over <what you actually read: their PRs, their commits, which repos>

## What they own
## What they have landed since <date>
## What of theirs touches our heads
## What they are waiting on
```

Written from what they did, not from what was said about them. A claim here that nobody re-read
is worse than an empty section, because it reads as current.
