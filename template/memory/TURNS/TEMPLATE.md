# `TURNS/<date>.md`

The turn journal, write-once: one section per routine that woke that day, in firing order. As
concise as the day can be said — it carries what *changed*, not the whole picture, and never a
table.

```markdown
# <YYYY-MM-DD>

## <phase emoji and name>, ~<HH:MM>–<HH:MM> UTC — <the one thing this turn was>

What changed, in a few lines. What was decided, and by whom. What was left undone, and why.
Links to the heads it touched, whose own state is in their `WORK/` files rather than here.
```

A turn whose work stayed inside one pull request writes its `WORK/` file and may write nothing
here. What lands here is what a *reader of the day* needs: rulings, surprises, what broke.
